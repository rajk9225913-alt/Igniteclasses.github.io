About Our Institute

Ignite Classes Sujangarh:
The Launchpad for Your Future

Located at the heart of Sujangarh (Churu District, Rajasthan), Ignite Classes was founded with a singular mission: to empower local and rural talent with top-tier, capital-grade competitive coaching right in their hometown—eliminating the need to migrate to expensive hubs like Jaipur, Sikar, or Jodhpur.

We provide structured, milestone-driven preparation for all major Rajasthan recruitment exams including CET (Senior Secondary & Graduation), REET (Level 1 & Level 2), Rajasthan Police Constable & Sub-Inspector, Patwari, Village Development Officer (VDO), Junior Accountant, LDC, RPSC 1st & 2nd Grade School Teachers, and BSTC/PTET.

Comprehensive Syllabus Coverage: Complete syllabus concluded with two rounds of thorough revisions and full-length simulated tests.

Daily Dedicated Doubt Counters: Subject faculty personally address conceptual doubts post-lecture every day.

Affordable & Transparent Fee Structure: Concessional fee models and installment facilities tailored for rural and middle-class families.
